## Truth and safety invariants

- C-level local/cloud observations never become formal A/B GEO evidence by convenience.
- Content generation does not equal publication.
- Publication does not equal indexing.
- Index submission does not equal indexing success.
- Publication, indexing or content generation do not equal GEO improvement.
- GEO improvement is accepted only from a new traceable A/B external-AI receipt.
- Financial actions remain outside autonomous execution.
